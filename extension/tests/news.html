Coastal cities publish a shared resilience plan

Mara Lewis · 09:30 HKT · June 28, 2026

Six coastal cities released a joint plan on Friday, creating a common framework for flood forecasting and emergency communication.

“The agreement gives local teams a shared language before the next storm arrives,” the report said.

Officials said the program will begin with public data and quarterly exercises. Funding decisions are expected later this year.
